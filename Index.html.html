List of Exercises Lv.1

Block 1: Shoulders
General Recommendations

Don't shrug your shoulders up toward your ears during the exercise. Try to keep your shoulders wide and your chest open. Keep your elbows bent at 90 degrees while doing the exercises. Your arms should stay relaxed. Watch your neck and facial muscles: they shouldn't tense up. The goal is isolation. All shoulder exercises are done using the chest and shoulder blade muscles.

100 times left shoulder round forward
100 times right shoulder round forward
100 times left shoulder round backward
100 times right shoulder round backward
Notes
In this part, the movements are wide and go round forward or backward. Keep your elbows almost still.
100 times left shoulder straight forward
100 times right shoulder straight forward
100 times left shoulder straight backward
100 times right shoulder straight backward
Notes
In this part, the movements are clear and go forward or backward. Do not make wide rotations. Keep your elbows almost still.
Block 2: Chest
100 times chest forward and up
Notes
The movement is strict. Do not use your shoulders to help the chest. Only the chest moves. Use your upper abs, not your back or shoulders. The movement should be light.
100 times chest to the left
100 times chest to the right
100 times chest in both directions
Notes
In this part, try to keep your chest in a straight, horizontal position. Do not bend.
20 times, pull your chest inward.
Notes
Try to pull your solar plexus inward, not your stomach, and don't just slouch. Imagine someone pressing hard on your solar plexus, or like you're trying to avoid touching something.
Block 3: Pelvis
100 times pelvis backward
100 times pelvis forward
Notes
In this part, only the pelvis works. Do not use your knees or legs.
Block 4: Legs
Stand on one leg. Find your balance. Extend the other leg forward.
20 times, rotate your foot outward.
20 times, rotate your foot inward.
(Notes)
Try to make the circular movement as smooth as possible, without any jerking.
Lift your thigh to a horizontal position.
20 times, rotate your lower leg outward.
20 times, rotate your lower leg inward.
(Notes)
Before the exercise, try to let your lower leg hang and relax it, including your foot. Ideally, the leg should hang completely loose, like it's not alive.
Lift your thigh higher.
20 times, rotate your thigh outward.
20 times, rotate your thigh inward.
(Notes)
The range of motion is important. The bigger, the better. But don't turn your knee outward more than 45°. Make sure your lower leg and foot stay relaxed.
20 times, rotate your lower leg inward.
(Notes)
Before the exercise, try to let your lower leg hang and relax it, including your foot. Ideally, the leg should hang completely loose, like it's not alive.
Lift your thigh higher.
20 times, rotate your thigh outward.
20 times, rotate your thigh inward.
(Notes)
The range of motion is important. The bigger, the better. But don't turn your knee outward more than 45°. Make sure your lower leg and foot stay relaxed.
Do the same for another leg
General Tips
  • You should feel uncomfortable when doing the exercises. If it feels easy, you are doing it wrong :) Closer to the 100th repetition, your muscles may feel tight. This is normal. Try not to stop the exercise.
  • Do not do the exercises fast. The slower and wider your movements, the better the result. If you can do everything quickly, you are not doing it well enough.
  • Rest between exercises. After hard shoulder exercises, shake your shoulders to relax the muscles.
  • Be patient. The first two weeks will be hard. It must be hard. Later, it will get better. Muscles that you do not usually use will grow, and new brain connections will form. You will feel a big difference on the dance floor after doing these exercises every day for even one month!
